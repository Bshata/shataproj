    Advanced Camera Troubleshooting

    Browser-specific Instructions

    Chrome

    1. Click the camera icon in the address bar
    2. Select "Allow" for camera access
    3. If not visible, go to chrome://settings/content/camera
    4. Add this site to the allowed sites list

    Firefox

    1. Click the camera icon in the address bar
    2. Click the "X" to remove the block
    3. Refresh the page and allow when prompted

    Edge

    1. Click the lock/info icon in the address bar
    2. Choose "Site permissions"
    3. Ensure camera is set to "Allow"

    System Settings

    Windows

    1. Press Windows+I to open Settings
    2. Go to Privacy & Security → Camera
    3. Make sure "Camera access" is On
    4. Ensure the option "Let apps access your camera" is On
    5. Check that your browser is allowed in the app list

    Mac

    1. Click Apple menu → System Preferences
    2. Click Security & Privacy → Privacy → Camera
    3. Ensure your browser is checked in the list

    Hardware Issues

    1. Try unplugging and reconnecting your camera if external
    2. Check if the camera works in other applications
    3. Check for camera indicator light to ensure it's powered
    4. Update camera drivers from manufacturer's website

    Camera Permission Required

    Please allow access to your camera when prompted by your browser.

    If you accidentally denied permission:

    1. Click the camera icon in your browser's address bar
    2. Select "Allow" for camera access
    3. Refresh the page
